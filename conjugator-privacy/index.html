Privacy Policy

Conjugator - Italian Verbs · Leeman Labs

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how Conjugator - Italian Verbs handles information when you use the Android app. For privacy questions, contact leemanlabs.apps@gmail.com.

Learning data and settings

The app stores learning progress, practice records, settings, reminder preferences, and purchase entitlement information in its private storage on your device. This information supports practice, progress tracking, reminders, and access to purchased features. The app does not upload your learning data to Leeman Labs or provide cloud synchronisation. App backup and device-transfer backup are disabled in the app configuration.

Google Play purchases

Optional Premium purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. Google handles payment details; the app does not receive your card number or bank details. The app receives purchase information, including a purchase token and signed purchase record, to verify, acknowledge, and restore Premium ownership. Verification and entitlement storage take place on your device; purchase acknowledgement and ownership checks use Google Play. Leeman Labs does not operate a purchase-verification server for the app.

Pronunciation and reminders

Pronunciation uses installed offline Italian voices through Android's text-to-speech service. The app does not record your voice or request microphone access. Optional practice reminders are scheduled locally on your device and use Android's notification permission where required.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, does not use an Advertising ID, and includes no analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell your app data or upload your learning activity to advertising or analytics services.

Retention, security, and deletion

Local app data is retained until you remove it. Android's application sandbox restricts other ordinary apps from accessing the app's private storage. You can erase local data using Android Settings → Apps → Conjugator → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app. This removes local learning progress and settings. Google Play may restore Premium ownership when you use the purchasing Google account; it does not restore your learning history.

Deleting local app data does not erase Google's purchase records or request a refund. Google's retention and deletion practices are governed by its own policies. The app has no separate Leeman Labs user account.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and retain it only as needed to handle the enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. To request deletion of support correspondence, email leemanlabs.apps@gmail.com.

Intended audience

The app is intended for learners aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page with an updated date.
