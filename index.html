Good to know

Is Conjugator free?

The complete verb reference and Essential Practice are free. An optional one-time Premium purchase opens practice across all 1000 verbs and 14 tenses. No subscription.

Does it need an internet connection?

Reference and practice work offline. Google Play needs a connection to buy Premium or check purchase ownership. Pronunciation uses an installed offline Italian voice.

Where is my learning progress stored?

On your device, in the app’s private storage. There’s no cloud sync. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes your learning progress and settings.
