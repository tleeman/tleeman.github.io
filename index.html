Good to know

Is Conjugator free?

Yes. The complete reference is free, with conjugations, examples and English explanations for all 1000 verbs and 14 tenses. Both Adaptive and Manual Practice are free for the Essential 100 verbs in Presente and Passato prossimo. An optional one-time Premium purchase opens practice across all 1000 verbs and 14 tenses. No subscription.

Does it need an internet connection?

Reference and practice work offline, including Premium practice once your purchase is confirmed. Google Play needs an internet connection to buy Premium or restore it after reinstalling or changing devices. Pronunciation works offline once an Italian voice is installed; downloading voice data may need a connection.

Where is my learning progress stored?

On your device, in the app’s private storage. There’s no cloud sync. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes your learning progress and settings.

Will Premium and my progress transfer to another device?

Premium can be restored through Google Play using the Google account you purchased it with and an internet connection. Conjugator checks ownership automatically when you open the app or the Premium sheet; you do not need to buy it again.

Learning progress and settings stay on the original device. There is no cloud sync or device-transfer backup, so restoring Premium does not restore your learning history. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes that history.

How do I enable Italian pronunciation?

Conjugator uses an installed offline Italian text-to-speech voice. In the app, open Settings and find Pronunciation. Use the Android speech settings option to install Italian voice data through your speech engine, then return to Conjugator to choose and preview a voice. The available voices and Android settings vary by device and speech engine.

Downloading voice data may need an internet connection. Once an offline Italian voice is installed, pronunciation works offline. Reference and practice remain available without a voice.
